
##### Definition:
These were the entities that sprung from the remains of the Greater Elementals. Colloquially known as Deities, these beings were much more plentiful than those who came before, and they held only fractions of the power of their progenitors. Only fractions of previous wisdom resided within them, too, leading to fighting between the Deities. The residual energy caused by the fighting is what sprung the first intelligent, mortal life. The constant battles caused the Deities to fracture themselves, shooting off shards of their energy around the Planes.
