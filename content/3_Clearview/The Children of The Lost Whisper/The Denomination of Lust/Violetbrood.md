#### Description
Violetbrood is a member of The [[Children of The Lost Whisper]].

Violetbrood works under the [[_Denomination of Lust |Denomination of Lust]].

"*Oh, please! If you would've let me handle it they would be ours! Next time, don't get in my fucking way.*"
- Violetbrood to [[Cutis]]

#### Lowly Acolytes
Violetbrood has formed a rocky alliance with other lowly acolytes of The [[Children of The Lost Whisper]]. 

[[Cutis]], the head of the group, seemingly calls the shots. Much to Violet's frustration.

Scab, a member of the Denomination of Wrath, knows his way around metalworking enough to rig together a van and some firearms.
![[van cultist 3 token.png]]

Venin, a thrall, seeks to complete basic tasks such as driving, scouting, and being a shield for the others.
