
#### Description
The Seedyr Family are the founders of the cult known as the [[Children of The Lost Whisper]]. 

Prominent Family Members:
- [[Maarx Seedyr]]; Grandfather and former patriarch
- [[Lora Seedyr]]; Grandmother and former failed vessel
	- [[Edward Seedyr]]; Brother to Maarx and former failed accomplice
	- [[Mikayla Seedyr]]; Wife to Edward and seeker of the dark
		- [[Tobi Seedyr]]; Son to Edward and taken too soon
	- [[Sean Seedyr]]; Son to Maarx and first to be reborn
	- [[Lithonia Ferrin]]; Partner to Sean and forever cursed
		- [[Nik Ferrin]]; Daughter to Lithonia and plagued with blight
		- [[Frank Ferrin]]; Son to Lithonia and unwilling subject to deathlessness
