#### Description
Maarx Panopeus Seedyr was the former leader of the [[Children of The Lost Whisper]]. Since the summoning of [[Desire]] and the splitting of denominations, he has been overthrown and exiled. 

*"Oh? You think you know what lies ahead? If you continue down this path, you and everything you hold dear will cease to exist. Not even your memory will be preserved, there will be nothing left capable of remembering."*

- Maarx to [[Ramathatus]], High Reverend of The Rotting Beast
