#### Description
The Hidden Whispers Grimoire is the written body of knowledge of its author, [[Maarx Seedyr]]. It contains *most* of what he knows, and the writings therein are of a most profane sort. 

"*That horrible black book. Its pages tainted with more than just ink, its contents tainted with more than mortal wrongdoing.*"
- [[Oblamog Ruiner]]

#### Sinister Emanations
Black, dried leather binds the old pages together. An eye sigil is imprinted into the cover, along with its title: *Liber Susurrorum Occultorum*.

The black book is 10" long by 8" wide and holds roughly 300 pages. 

A malignant aura follows the black book. Undoubtably, a reservoir of [[Essence]] pools within. 

#### Occult Knowledge
The contents of the black book are categorized within groups:
1. *Whispers of the [[Desire |Sequestered One]]* - the transcriptions of Desire
2. *Occult Sightings in [[Clearview]]* - a list of beings spotted within the Village of the Occult
3. *[[_Deity |Deities]] of The Northern [[The Highlands |Highlands]]* - dissertations concerning the Deities, their origin, behaviors, and abilities
