#### Hailstorm of Anguish
While trapped in [[Feylocke]], [[The Party]] was told that the closest way to return to the Material Plane was to find [[Daedalus]], a legendary Dwarven blacksmith. Only he had the tools to open standing portals between Planes that could support all four members of [[The Party]]. He was located within a mountain not too far away, but the journey to get to him would be perilous. One such danger being a newly formed hailstorm that moved in unpredictable ways. There were scant reports of people entering into the storm and going missing. *Some even claimed that they heard screaming coming from within*.

[[The Party]] set out on their quest to return home. After some troubles, they were in the final stretch. Less than a day's travel was left before they made it to the base of Daedalus' mountain. One final challenge stood in their way: the hailstorm was between them and the mountain. A choice had to be made. Either they would travel around the storm's edge, risking being caught out come nightfall. Or, they would brave through the storm, confronting whatever malign influence that laid within. With a dangerous beast on their tail, and the sun threatening to set quickly, [[The Party]] decided to venture within the hailstorm. 

Hail buffeted [[The Party]] as they pushed forward. A near complete white-out. Thick, white snow and hail blotted out the sky, the temperate weather of the day replaced with a biting cold and numbing winds. As The Party continued forward, they saw a clearing where the hailstorm was much less fierce. The eye of the storm was a brief respite. Until they spotted a creature standing in the center of the clearing. 
![[Freezing Man Token.png]]
As [[The Party]] started to walk around the edges of the clearing, it began to show signs of aggression. The frozen creature wailed, and in response, icy spirits manifested around each party member. These spirits wanted nothing more than to subjugate each party member, and they used emotional manipulation in an attempt to force them into despair. 

"*Samuel Cask. [[Aduro]]. The fledgling. Tries to change the system but fails at every turn. Nothing but a vermin, scuttling around with delusions of grandeur. Gaze upon the meaningless destruction in your wake. Remember your true place in this world, and know that the system will stand tall over your body.*
*Do you think you can change anything now?*"
