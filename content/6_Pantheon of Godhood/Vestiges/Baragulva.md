#### Description
Baragulva is a [[_Vestige |Vestige]] of [[Folm]].
Baragulva is bound to XX. 

"*To hunger is to live, no matter how predatory.*"
- Baragulva 

His physical form is that of depraved insatiability. A toothed maw engraved upon his chest, used to devour all unlucky enough to be caught in his path. Pulsating muscle takes any shape he deems necessary for the feast.  

#### Vessel of Baragulva
Baragulva's plans were altered when a child fell under the influence of [[Folm]]. The child was so young yet shone with opportunity. Those born under such circumstances were anomalies, and Baragulva was not about to let it slip between his bloody fingers. 

The consumption of another at such an early part of life left the door open for any number of Folmic Entities to enter. Baragulva was the first to see the babe. He quickly manifested himself to it, and explained simply that it wanted to care for it. Of course, the baby knew not of what or how he spoke, and trusted him blindly. Thus, Baragulva protected the child himself from mortals and other Vestiges of Folm. 

When the child began to grow, and question, and feast, Baragulva presented an option that would mutually benefit the two of them. The child would allow him in, and in turn, they would navigate the world together and feast. Vestiges of Folm would no longer be drawn to the child if he existed from within. So, seeing that it was either being a vessel or surely dying to the increasingly intense waves of Folmic Vestiges or mortals claiming the child to be a demon, the pact was made.
