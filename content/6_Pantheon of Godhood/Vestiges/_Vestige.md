
#### Definition
A Vestige is a being who inherits power directly from a [[_Deity]] or [[_Greater Elemental]]. These beings can be created in a variety of ways. Some are created by their patron, others are transformed by their patron, willingly or otherwise. Vestiges vary greatly in power and knowledge, as some are more closely tied to their patron, making them more powerful. There is no crystal or item of which a Vestige uses to channel their magic, that is what separates these beings from Mortals who wield shards of Deities.
