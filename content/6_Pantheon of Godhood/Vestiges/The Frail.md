#### Description
The Frail are [[ _Vestige]]s of Strife.

"*They don't need to be strong, they just need to incite fear.*"
- [[Gilded Masks]] Member Aawec 

Excerpt from The [[_Hidden Whispers Grimoire]]:
	"...Lowly, pitiful creatures. These beings are created out of nothing but [[Strife]]'s essence. With no physical material to cling to, they end up being quite weak. Since these creatures possess an incredibly small fraction of His essence, they cannot perform any magics that are usual of those under [[Strife]]'s influence. However, they often swarm in groups, and should not be underestimated in such a circumstance..."

They are classified as a Vestige due to their direct connection to their patron.

Slain by [[The Party]].
