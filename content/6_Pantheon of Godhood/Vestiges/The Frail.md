#### Description
The Frail are [[ _Vestige]]s of Strife.

"*They don't need to be strong, they just need to incite fear.*"

Lowly, pitiful creatures. These beings are created out of nothing but Strife's essence, which was in high concentrations within The Pit. With no physical material to cling to, they end up being quite weak. Since The Frail only possess an incredibly small fraction of essence, they cannot perform any magics that are usual of those under Strife's influence. However, they often swarm in groups, and should not be underestimated in such a circumstance. 

They are classified as a Vestige due to their direct connection to their patron.
