#### Description
The Disturbed is a [[ _Vestige]] of Strife.

"*I'm going down there. Those golden-masked freaks are hiding the good stuff I just know it*."

- Last words of Marcus Thistle

Excerpt from The [[Hidden Whispers Grimoire]]:
	"...It seems that [[Strife]]'s influence manifests in just as strange of ways as the other Spawn of Anathet. We all know what He wants, so its reasonable to expect areas with high rates of violence to be more fertile grounds for his influence to take shape. These areas stink of *sulfur*, an olfactory detail that only seems to be present when His influence is concentrated enough. Perhaps those masks the [[Collectors]] use is why they never seem to notice it, or maybe I am more sensitive to it..."
