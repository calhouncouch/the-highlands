#### Description
The Hopeful is a [[ _Vestige]] of Strife.

"*I thought I heard someone in distress over here? Someone is? Please, the four of you, lead the way so we can help them!*"
- Hailey Ayea to the [[Gilded Masks]] 

Excerpt from The [[Hidden Whispers Grimoire]]:
	...A striking feature of such areas desecrated by [[Strife]] is the presence of fleshy growths, hereby referred to as *Sarcous Cradles*. Their dimensions vary, seemingly based on what their innards contain. From what I have seen, it looks like conscious, organic materials are brought into the desecrated area and a ritual is performed on them. If successful, they are consumed by occult flesh and this results in a Sarcous Cradle. Strangely enough, the more willing a subject is to undergo this ritual, the less it seems to work. Perhaps the Sarcous Cradle must absorb some kind of discontent for it to form properly..."

Slain by [[The Party]].
