
### <span style="color:rgb(70, 130, 180)"><i>The Lady of the Forge</i></span>
Pronounced: MIN UR VAY
Color: Steel Blue (#4682B4 or 70, 130, 180)

*Minervae taught mortals how to work the metals of the earth, her only law; create for creations' sake.*

#### Place in the Pantheon
Minervae is a [[_Deity]]. 
Her essence originated from:
- [[Astrula]], *The Universal Constant*

Her [[_Vestige]]s are:
- [[Vae Ilmenite]]
- [[Vae Mare]]
- [[Vae Pyroxene]]
- [[Vae Regolith]]
- [[Vae Thorium]]
Minervic Vestiges are also known as *Divine Constructions*. 

Known Users of her essence:
- [[Dulrum]]

#### Spells
Damage types associated with Minervae, The Lady of the Forge are:
- Bludgeoning
- Cold
- Fire
- Force
- Lightning
- Thunder
