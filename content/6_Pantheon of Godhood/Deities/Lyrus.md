#### Rites, Rituals, and Prayers

**Rite of Lyrain Passage**

To complete the rite, you will need:
- A crystal of Lyrus in any form,
- A source of power comparable to a lightning strike,
- A small holding apparatus,
- Ritual chalk,
- An unobstructed vertical or horizontal surface that is 10 ft x 10 ft in dimension,
- To be within the light of a full moon. 
Once you have everything, you are ready to begin.

**First**, fix your holding apparatus upright, and ensure the Eye is securely held in place.
**Second**, ensure that the the path from the holding apparatus to your 10 x 10 surface is unobstructed. 
**Third**, using the ritual chalk, draw a circle with a 10 ft diameter on your flat surface, then draw a circle around your holding apparatus. Connect the two with a straight line.
**Fourth**, apply power to the Eye. You will know it is working if the Eye glows with a bright yellow after the power has been applied to it.
**Fifth**, recite the following incantation to draw out Lyrus' power from the Eye:

*From the power invested in Eloneloae,*
*The Great Seed of Choice,*
*I summon thee, Unseen Trickster*
*Through your ineffable ways,*
*I alone see you clearly this night*
*Grant me Planar passage*
*For under the glow of your moonlight*
*Anything is possible.*

If done correctly, a yellow current of energy should be flowing to the circle drawn in step three. That circle is your standing portal to Feylocke. Be wary, the portal will be open for all to enter until the power current or surface area of the portal is disrupted.
