#### The Twelve Rings of Zealotry
The God of Giants was a jeweler during his time. He was often secluded from his siblings, preferring the solitary nature of his interests. [[Minervae]] was the only company he enjoyed.  
  
In his solitude, he crafted many fine pieces of jewelry. Necklaces, earrings, and most importantly, rings. Some say his fascination for rings came from him having six fingers on each hand, a trait that he did not share with his siblings. Days on end would be spent in his craft, many rings were discarded for not meeting his standards. Some of which were instead collected by [[Minervae]]. Eventually, twelve rings were complete. An entire set for himself. 

However, when his self-imposed task was complete, Cavernicus realized that his time in isolation came with a price. He saw exploitation, deceit, and wrongdoing. Mortals needed someone to save them, and he saw that no one was coming. His rings then took on a different use. Each one was engraved with a special weapon, some of his design, others of [[Minervae]]'s. Together, they created the **Twelve Rings of Zealotry**.  
  
These rings are incredibly powerful, each one housing a deadly armament. To access the weapon within, *you must either be of Giant descent or be fueled by a righteous cause*. If either are not met, the weapon will not show itself to it's wielder.
