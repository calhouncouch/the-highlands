
### <span style="color:rgb(255, 237, 41)"><i>The Seed of Choice</i></span>
Pronounced: EL IN OH WAY
Color: Bright Yellow (#FFED29 or 255, 237, 41)

*The gift of free will was granted to mortals by Eloneloae.* 

#### Place in the Pantheon 
Eloneloae is a [[Greater Elemental]].
From their essence came:
- [[Cavernicus]], The Weapons of the Betrayed
- [[Desire]], The Conduit of Mortal Desire
- [[Mesenrie]], The Free Spirit
- [[Liligothra]], The Tempting Snare
- [[Lyrus]], The Unseen Trickster

#### Spells
Damage types associated with Eloneloae, *The Seed of Choice* are:
- Force
- Lightning
- Psychic
- Radiant
- Thunder
