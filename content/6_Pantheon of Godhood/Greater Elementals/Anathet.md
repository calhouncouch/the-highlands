
### <span style="color:rgb(0, 0, 0)"><i>Entropy Eternal</i></span>
Pronounced: ANN AH THET
Color: Black (#000000 or 0,0,0)

*Anathet holds all things subject to change and disorder.*

#### Place in the Pantheon
Anathet is a [[_Greater Elemental]].
From their essence came:
- [[Desire]], *The Conduit of Mortal Desire*
- [[Strife]], *The Wings of Moral Strife*
- [[Rot]], *The Inevitability of Mortal Rot*
- [[Quietus]], *The Faces of Mortal Death*

#### Spells
The damage types associated with Anathet, *Entropy Eternal* are:
- Acid
- Cold
- Fire
- Force
- Necrotic
- Poison
- Psychic
