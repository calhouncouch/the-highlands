
#### Definition
Greater Elementals were the first appearance of life in any of the planes, representing the primordial forces that shape reality. Their creation is unknown. Incredibly wise and powerful, they roamed the Material Plane. As time went on, they began to break down. Pieces of those Elementals spread across the Material Plane, and from them, new life began to emerge. That new life was the [[_Deity]](s).

#### Categorization

There existed five Greater Elementals. It is said they are equal in power, however, in modern times that statement is held in contention. 

The five Greater Elementals:
1. [[Suturei]], *The Spring of Life*
2. [[Mopathe]], *Destruction Incarnate*
3. [[Anathet]], *Entropy Eternal*
4. [[Astrula]], *The Universal Constant*
5. [[Eloneloae]], *The Seed of Choice*
