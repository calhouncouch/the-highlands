
#### Description
The Black Powder are a gang within [[Corininth]] who specialize in making firearms. 

"*Hold your positions and keep your head. We'll outpace anyone standing in our way.*"
- [[Horus]]

#### Membership Ranks
There exist three rankings of membership within the Black Powder:
1. *Dissipator* - the leaders of the Black Powder
2. *Carbine* - experienced members who have interest in furthering Black Powder aims
3. *Member* - recruits or affiliates who have no interest in climbing the ranks

To become a Carbine, one must be chosen by a Carbine or Dissipator and undergo a special rite of passage. The inductee is escorted to a sanctuary where they are instructed to create a piece of technology unique to them. Guns, gadgets, explosives, or other tools are valid for this process. 

The sanctuary assigned to [[Corininth]] is [[Hollow Point]].
