
#### Description
Is the medical company that runs the hospital within [[Corininth]]. 

"*Its dangerous out there. Don't go trying to get yourself killed, here, take this.*"
- Renee, Head Nurse of Hospice 

#### Characters
BioImmune, and its subsidiary force The Collectors, have many employees. Notably:
- [[Jess]], former Collector 
- Leo, current Collector
- Renee, the Head Nurse of Hospice

#### Security
The main BioImmune building has a unique defense system:
*The Chilopoda Putrescens*. 

![[The Centipede Token.png]]
A being comprised of flesh and metal, being controlled by a central server housed within the building. Its directive being to defend the sensitive substances that are stored at this location.
