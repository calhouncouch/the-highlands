#### Operation Cluster
The first set of robots made public were the *Arrays*. 

Arrays were split into two groups: DX Arrays and EX Arrays. 

DX Arrays were created to automate menial tasks, such as physical labor and maintenance. Their frames varied between individual models and usually looked more machine than humanoid.

EX Arrays were created for expanding the borders of the [[Corininth |Great City]]. They held much higher intellectual capacities than their DX counterparts, and as such, these Arrays could employ diplomacy. Machine ambassadors were often used to contact other settlements, collect debts, and spy for information. When they failed, though, other EX Arrays would take their positions. Often referred to as Executioners, these Arrays were walking weapons. Most EX Arrays were constructed of humanoid frames.

*The construction of Arrays has halted*. After the acquisition of [[LEVI |EX-KHWS-LVI]] and his integration into the Continuous Array Matrix, a latent bio-mechanical trojan was released into the network. This anomalous program quickly copied itself and latched onto each Array, gifting them with consciousness above their programming limitations. With this new sense, *most* Arrays revolted against their creators and *Operation Cluster* disastrously ended.


#### Project Steel Hammer
After the dismantling of Operation Cluster, and its coveted Continuous Array Matrix, Providence swiftly began work on a new project. It was similar to their previous endeavor, *but this time a new Patron took interest in their creations*.

New robots and exo-suits would be constructed, now referred to as *War Machines*. 

These models would be much more individualized. An overarching network does not connect them, instead, they have been programed from the ground up with a similar consciousness to the bio-mechanical trojan. A new power source has been utilized for many of these models, its steel-blue hue beaming bright with potential.
