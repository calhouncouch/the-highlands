Was a member of the [[_Oikia Tribe]].

- Izitli Shendal 

Character Theme: *You Know Nothing of Honor* | [WE ON GO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zc4r7GGXAvw&list=RDZc4r7GGXAvw&start_radio=1) by Bia

Izitli Shendal, son of Adaman Shendal, is an experienced weaponsmith. His youth was spend in the Oikia Armory or in the Temple of Giants. The furnace fires burned with less intensity than his belief in [[Cavernicus]], *The Weapons of the Betrayed*. Beliefs of legacy, blood-ties, and honor shaped him into a fierce warrior. Even at a young age, he launched himself into battle after battle. 

#### Holding the Royal-Purple Flame
Izitli was only fourteen when the Oikic Slaughter campaign ended. His guilt over not being able to protect those he deemed under his care festered for years.
These feelings only reinforced his need to become stronger, not only physically, but through his connection to his God. Izitli trained. For many long years, he remained isolated and honed his abilities. While long-suffering, his regimen did yield results. 

*The Thousand Pierced Blade*, his macahuitl, was the weapon he chose to wield. 
Hickory wood composes the weapon's body, and Izitli fashioned razor-sharp pieces of obsidian to act as the blade. These glass-like fragments are both incredibly sharp and efficient to replace. Most metals can be burned down into this obsidian, so Izitli could easily replace the shards by using materials of felled enemies. Furthermore, if pressure is applied to the obsidian shards at a certain angle, they could be broken off of the weapon and embedded into its target. This tactic is extremely painful and often created an opening for Izitli to land the finishing blow. 

As Izitli's experience grew, he realized he had forged a unique connection to The Thousand Pierced Blade. All of his study on [[Cavernicus]] and yet he was not fully aware of the Essence that flowed from him to the blade and vis versa. Eventually, through much reflection and further study of Occultism, he realized that this connection can grant many advantages. So, he began to learn new techniques, one of which being the *Royal-Purple Portent*. It was a simple trick which utilized the innate connection between himself and his weapon. By focusing on the handle of his blade, he could quickly travel to it and wield it. The movement is so fast it looks like teleportation. However, it requires a sound mind to perform, and Izitli does not gain sight of his weapon mid-flight. This weakness was shown when an opponent displaced the weapon while he was traveling to it and he was caught the blast.

Being a Goliath, Izitli's birthright is the ancient *Renewal of Divinity*. An ability where he assumes a larger form and becomes much more powerful. This hereditary ability slightly differs between the Elemental Ancestries of the Giants. For Izitli, being a descendent of the Fire Giants, his Renewal wreathes him in purple flame.
