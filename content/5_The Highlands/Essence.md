
#### Description
*Essence is the force of one's will.* 

Every living thing contains Essence. It is housed within one's very soul, and it is the defining variable that separates each living thing from each other. Think of it as your Spiritual Fingerprint, its form, function, and abilities greatly vary from individual to individual. 

#### Divine Essence
The [[_Greater Elemental]]s themselves were formed entirely of their Essence. 

How they came into being has been debated for as long as Mortals can remember, and it is likely to never truly be known. However, it is undeniable that they have existed and that their power is the most concentrated and intense that have ever existed. Their descendants, the [[_Deity | Deities]], inherit many of their incredible abilities. 


------------

[[_Deity |Deities]] are formed entirely out of their Essence.

While being a step down from their ancestors, these beings still carry immense power. This strength stemming from their deep wells of Essence that they use to perform what laypeople call *magic*. 

Due to each Deity's unique composition from their ancestors, their Essence's varied. Take [[Mavorus]] and [[Kolasi]] for example. They both trace their origins back to [[Mopathe]], but, where [[Mavorus]] is only drawn from Him, [[Kolasi]] draws power from [[Astrula]] as well. This difference in origin is the main explanation as to why their power and symbolism differ. 

You may ask, then, how do [[Mavorus]] and [[Folm]] differ? They both draw their Essences from [[Mopathe]] *and* [[Astrula]]. The common answer is that they draw Essence from their originators at different ratios. [[Folm]]'s Essence is more based in [[Astrula]] than [[Mopathe]], and [[Mavorus]] vis versa.

However, there is one oddity to this theory: *Why do the Essences of [[Eloneloae]]'s offspring vary when some only draw their origins from him?* Why and how is [[Mesenrie]], [[Liligothra]], and [[Lyrus ]]all different when they draw their power from the same source? This question is debated today, but, one theory that many posit is that Eloneloae's Essence is simply more varied and complex than His [[_Greater Elemental]] siblings. 

#### Mortal Essence
Mortals, being living creatures, possess their own Essence.

Each Mortal's Essence is individually different. It is housed within the soul and works through their body. Spiritual groups are often founded to study and experiment with how Mortal Essences work. The amount of Essence Mortals possess is very small compared to most Spiritual Beings such as [[_Vestige |Vestiges]], [[_Deity |Deities]], and [[_Greater Elemental |Greater Elementals]]. 

The way Mortals usually go about increasing their power through their Essence is by using Spiritual Beings as a lens to focus their Essence through. Such Spiritual Beings often being the [[_Deity | Deities]]. This can be done in a variety of ways:
- venerating a [[_Deity | Deity]] and currying their favor
- finding an item that has been imbued with a [[_Deity | Deity]]'s essence, most often being a shard of the respective [[_Deity | Deity]]
- seeking pacts with a [[_Deity | Deity]] and becoming their [[_Vestige | Vestige]]

However, it is often the case that using a [[_Deity | Deity]] to magnify one's own ability comes with unforeseen consequences. Some more subtle than others. 


------------------

Mortals can also strengthen their own Essence on their own. Deepening their wells of Essence without a spiritual intermediary. The process of consciously strengthening one's own Essence is *very difficult*. Processes themselves have varied between Mortals, as each Mortal's Essence is unique to them, and such processes often come with a cost. Whether it be physical such as strenuous physical labors or fasting, or mental such as abstinence and isolation. Most fail before the first step of their growth can begin, as they are completely unaware of the fact that they house Essence within them in the first place.
