#### Tender Is The Flesh
Hergan Dosk's birth was that of tragedy. Having clawed his way into the world, he was left alone and vulnerable. A smallfolk's life was already marked with hardship, and due to the circumstances of his birth, the difficulties to come would only be greater. The lonely babe did not cry, nor did he understand what was surely to come. *His hunger was satiated and that is what mattered*.

The [[Brimstone Crags]] is an unforgiving place. Between its jagged rock, perilous pitfalls, and noxious fumes, anyone caught unprepared will surely meet their end. Those born here must be protected with the utmost care. Dosk was quite fortunate to be found by The [[Order of the Maw]] before he could have died. Though, the members who came across Dosk witnessed a scene reminiscent of ancient murals: an infant sat next to a pile of corpses.
