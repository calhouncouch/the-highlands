#### Associations
Is a member of [[The Party]].
Is a smallfolk hailing from [[Feylocke]]. 
Raised in [[Corininth]], has rocky connections with the three smallfolk guilds of the city.
- The [[Alchemist's Guild]]
- The [[Apothecarist's Guild]]
- The [[Mechanaut's Guild]]

Had a childhood friend, [[Tinner]], of which they grew apart after years of absence.
Is the Guild Master of his own new guild: [[The Scrappers Guild]].
