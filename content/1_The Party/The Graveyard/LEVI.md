
#### Description
EX-KHWS-LVI (LEVI) was an [[_Array]] built by [[_Providence]]. 

"*Greetings, EX-KHWS-LVI.* 

*Welcome back. We regret to inform you that your time in stasis will be over indefinitely as of September 28th, 20XV. You stand as our last bastion of defense against those who seek to destroy us and our great city. While you were away, an anomalous bio-mechanical trojan was unleashed upon Providence’s Machine Arrays, leading to a battle that we were not ready for. The Arrays went haywire, forfeiting their duties and turning against us. We were able to capture some, and some came back willingly, but others managed to escape our grasp.*

*Your primary mission is to find and return your fellow Arrays to us. Due to their increased awareness, you may use diplomacy if needed. However, it is fully expected that most will not come back willingly, and in those cases, full force is authorized to neutralize these threats. Their cores are the most important part to capture, and you have full authority to utilize their components after they have been defeated, should you see fit.* 

*Your secondary mission is to scout for information. If you find intel on those who may be plotting against us, it is imperative that you record these events in your Internal Logging System. We will be scanning and repairing you upon every return visit to our headquarters.* 

*Your tertiary missions are left up to you. You are given full control to pursue potentially threatening leads, and you are granted full amnesty for collateral damages that may arise from these situations.* 

*You are our most valuable asset. Should you fail your missions, our great city may fall to forces that seek to destroy us. You have been given incredible ability and flexibility in pursuing these missions, and we have full confidence that you will succeed. Use any means necessary to complete your duties. Should you have any questions, return back to headquarters.*"
- [[Maestro]], Head of Research and Design at [[ _Providence ]]
