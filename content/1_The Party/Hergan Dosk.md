#### Tender Is The Flesh
Hergan Dosk's birth was that of tragedy. Having clawed his way into the world, he was left alone and vulnerable. A smallfolk's life was already marked with hardship, and due to the circumstances of his birth, the difficulties to come would only be greater. The lonely babe did not cry, nor did he understand what was surely to come. *His hunger was satiated and that is what mattered*.

The [[_Brimstone Crags | Brimstone Crags]] is an unforgiving place. Between its jagged rock, perilous pitfalls, and noxious fumes, anyone caught unprepared will surely meet their end. Those who live here must be protected with the utmost care. Dosk was quite fortunate to be found by The [[Order of the Maw]] before he could have died. Though, the members who came across Dosk witnessed a scene reminiscent of ancient murals: a sleeping infant next to a pile of corpses. 

A lingering presence was felt by the small group of witnesses. Something ravenous, something... *protective*? They gently held the babe, wondering what could have possibly happened to cause this. The child's demeanor unphased by the surrounding carnage, all the while an unseen force permeated the area. The members quickly fled with the child, *and the hungering presence followed*.

Once the group returned to their sanctuary, they brought the recovered child to their leader, Hyn Faranaias. He clearly saw the child's affliction, *appetence*, one that was felt among each of the members of his order. Seeing a child as young and as innocent as Dosk be consumed by such a misfortune opened his heart. So the order came to their verdict: to raise the child and study the presence that follows him.
